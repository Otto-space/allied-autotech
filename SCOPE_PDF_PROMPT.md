Create a polished, owner-facing PDF for my Allied AutoTech project scope and fee discussion.

Read these files first:

1. `ORIGINAL_CLIENT_BRIEF.md` — the original requirements I supplied for comparison with the NGN 800,000 quote.
2. `SCOPE_EVOLUTION_BRIEF.md` — the evidence-backed comparison, qualifications, development history and proposed discussion wording.

Use the available PDF skill. Produce `output/pdf/Allied_AutoTech_Scope_Review.pdf` and an editable Markdown companion with the exact final PDF copy. Render and inspect every PDF page, fixing clipping, bad line wrapping, crowded tables, missing glyphs, page-number errors and awkward page breaks before handing it over. Preserve existing files; do not change application code, buy anything, publish anything or contact anyone.

Audience: a business owner, not a software engineer. Tone: calm, professional, transparent and collaborative. Suggested length: 6–8 readable A4 pages, adjusting as needed for legibility. Use restrained navy, white and red; an existing approved Allied AutoTech logo if available; clear typography, comfortable margins, page numbers and a small evidence appendix. Do not invent signatures, approvals, seals or a company identity for the developer.

Suggested content:

1. Title: “Allied AutoTech — Scope Review and Proposed Completion Agreement”; date; “For discussion — not an invoice.”
2. Executive explanation acknowledging that the original brief already included a full functional platform.
3. Original commitments, grouped into business functions.
4. Candidate extensions E1–E7, explaining original promise, later extension, business value, overlap and current status. Use readable cards or short tables rather than one oversized table.
5. Original-scope completion, ordinary quality fixes, design refinements and superseded service-deposit requirements. Explain these fairly and briefly.
6. Remaining policies, implementation and deployment dependencies; distinguish local acceptance from public readiness.
7. An unfilled decision/approval worksheet: keep now, defer or already included; agreed revised total; payments credited; milestone payments; delivery acceptance; support period; exclusions.
8. Short evidence appendix with repository-relative source paths and verified commit IDs. These are supporting development records, not proof of commercial consent.

Mandatory accuracy rules:

- Invoice viewing is original scope; PDF downloads/sharing are the candidate extension. Vehicle enquiries/inspections are original scope; reservation transactions and exact agreement acceptance are the candidate extension.
- Ordinary security, payment correctness, bug fixes and baseline responsive quality must not be presented as optional paid upgrades.
- Mention that the original service-deposit requirement was subsequently replaced by no-deposit bookings, subject to the recorded policy evidence.
- Do not mark unfinished marketing/privacy execution, unapproved reservation terms, cloud hosting or real storage/provider acceptance as complete.
- Keep the main commercial worksheet blank. If mentioning NGN 1.5 million, identify it only as the developer's proposed revised total, a NGN 700,000 increase, subject to agreement. Do not invent feature-by-feature prices, hours worked, amounts already paid, acceptance dates or revenue guarantees.
- The signed owner-policy checklist was described by a repository matrix; do not claim to have inspected the signed PDF unless you actually do so.
- Hosting subscriptions and ongoing maintenance are separate commercial items; no purchase is approved. Do not restate pricing as a fresh provider quote without checking its date and sources.
- Use NGN where a reliable naira glyph is unavailable. No secrets, private account identifiers or customer records.

Conclude by giving me the local PDF and editable-copy links, the page count, and any material uncertainty retained. Do not remove honest qualifications to make the sales argument stronger.
