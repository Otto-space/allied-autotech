# Reservation agreement policy workspace

GET `/admin/policies/reservation-terms/current` returns evaluatedAt, current, nextScheduled, latest and the latest 100 recorded revisions. Effective and next scheduled selections scan all matching policy versions independently from that bounded history. A consistent repeatable snapshot, current actor/session lock, Super Admin/MFA, strict empty query, private/no-store response and minimal atomic RESERVATION_POLICY_CURRENT READ audit protect access.

Publication now includes VEHICLE_RESERVATION_TERMS in the current actor/session check before the existing policy advisory lock, expected-version comparison, immutable Q3 revision and CREATE audit. Customer acceptance retains that same policy lock and immutable document association. No schema/migration is needed; historical agreement text, customer acceptance and financial policies are unchanged.

The existing discriminated settings remain authoritative. Enabled agreements require plain-text title (1–200 characters) and body (1–50,000); withdrawal requires reason (1–1,000). Unknown settings fields and prohibited control characters are rejected. Required source/evidence and future-or-immediate publication semantics are unchanged. A withdrawal does not cancel later scheduled revisions: a later enabled revision can restore availability. Agreement availability does not activate payment/deposit functionality.

The dashboard presents current facts, full text, scheduling and bounded history beside separate publish/withdraw fields. It retains action drafts and their original base across reload, requires fresh approval consent, validates exact Q3/actor/body receipts and keeps uncertain outcomes locked without replay. Durable original-request receipt lookup remains ADM-63 work; current-state inspection cannot prove an interrupted request's original outcome.
