check email status and keep an accurate case history. The website currently accepts
requests requiring staff confirmation. It does not collect payments or guarantee
an appointment, inspection allocation, product order or vehicle reservation.

## Start with the case

1. Sign in using your own authorised account. Open Support → Enquiries or
   Complaints in your dashboard. Administrator entry points are
   https://alliedautotech.com/admin/support/enquiries and
   https://alliedautotech.com/admin/support/complaints. Other staff should use the
   support navigation provided to their role; do not request Super Admin access
   simply to handle support.
2. Check the reference, customer, branch, subject, linked service/product/vehicle
   and previous messages. Match the email reference to the case. Do not disclose
   another customer's records based only on an unverified email claim.
3. Identify the person handling the request and acknowledge it within the target.
   For complaints, use the dedicated acknowledgement action when available; an
   ordinary reply or Gmail message does not by itself record the application's
   complaint acknowledgement timestamp.
4. Check actual price, stock or workshop availability before promising an outcome.
   Record agreed visit arrangements in the company's appointment process as well
   as the support case. Changing support status does not allocate a slot.

## Buttons and customer visibility

Opening a Review screen previews the proposed action. It takes effect only after
the final confirmation. Read the action and visibility in that confirmation.

| Confirmed action | What happens | Customer email |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status change | Updates the case and customer-visible status history | Queues an update, subject to delivery eligibility |
| Status change with a resolution/explanation | Saves the status and explanation together | One combined update; do not repeat the same resolution through Reply |
| Customer-visible reply | Adds a response the customer can access | Queues an update, subject to delivery eligibility |
| Internal staff note | Records information for authorised staff | No customer email |
| Assignment change | Changes the handling staff member | No customer email |
| Complaint priority change | Changes handling priority and applicable deadline calculation | No customer email from this action |
| First complaint acknowledgement | Records acknowledgement and a customer-visible message | Queues an update; repeating an already-recorded acknowledgement does not create another one |

Typical labels are **Review status change**, **Review assignment change**,
**Review reply** and **Review internal note**. An older screen may group actions
under **Review Support Change**: the selected action determines its effect.

For registered customers, email alerts direct them to sign in and read their
conversation. Guest emails include the supplied response/explanation and allow
ordinary mailbox replies. Customer-visible messages are not public website posts.

## Case status and closure

Enquiries progress from Open to In progress to Resolved to Closed. Complaints use
Investigating instead of In progress. The application also permits moving directly
from Open to Resolved where appropriate.

- **Open:** awaiting handling.
- **In progress / Investigating:** someone is actively handling it; this does not
  promise an appointment, payment, refund or completed investigation.
- **Resolved:** the substantive response/action has been provided. Leave room for
  further correspondence while outstanding questions are addressed.
- **Closed:** correspondence is finished. Current controls prevent further replies
  from both staff and customers, including staff notes, and provide no reopening
  action. Record necessary follow-up before closing; do not close just to clear
  the queue. If a closed case needs follow-up, consult the lead and use a new
  appropriately linked request rather than altering history or bypassing controls.

Closing is not deletion. It does not itself prove that every retention obligation
has ended or that a customer's issue has been resolved satisfactorily.

## Check Customer email delivery

After confirming a customer-visible action, use **Refresh email delivery**.
The list shows the latest updates, newest first; it is not the complete mailbox
history. Older messages are not backfilled.

| Status | Meaning | Staff action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Queued | Waiting for the worker | Allow processing and refresh; do not create a duplicate reply |
| Sending | An attempt is in progress | Refresh before any manual follow-up |
| Sent | The email provider accepted the send | This does not prove inbox placement or reading |
| Retrying | Another attempt is scheduled | Do not resend the same reply manually while automatic delivery is pending |
| Failed | Automatic delivery stopped | Ask the technical administrator to inspect the job/provider result before coordinating one follow-up |
| Not sent | A prerequisite or customer preference prevented sending | Read the reason and follow the guidance below |

If status is unavailable, refresh and ask the technical administrator to inspect
the outcome before retrying. Repeatedly saving the same customer reply can create
duplicates. A successful save and a successful email send are separate events.

For **Not sent**:

- Customer turned off operational emails: the response remains in their account.
  Do not bypass their preference by manually emailing the same update.
- No recipient: use another contact method the customer supplied for this request,
  where appropriate, and record the outcome.
- Email disabled or reply address missing: report the configuration issue to the
  technical administrator. Agree one manual follow-up only after checking the
  existing queue and customer preferences.
- Testing allowlist restriction: report the environment issue; do not broaden an
  allowlist or change launch settings yourself.

Technical retries belong to an authorised operator. Staff should not change
provider settings or resend a whole case to try to repair an individual email.

## Replying in the Support mailbox

Automated notifications come from `notifications@notify.alliedautotech.com`.
Human replies should come from `support@alliedautotech.com`. General, sales and
support website alerts currently all route to Support.

The mailbox workflow previously verified with the company is Google Groups →
Support → Conversations. For a website alert, **Reply to author** was observed
to target the automated sender. Use **Reply all**, then inspect the recipients:

1. Set From to `support@alliedautotech.com`.
2. Ensure the correct customer is in To or Cc; Cc was tested successfully. Check
   against the case and the message's Reply-To information, not the display name.
3. Remove the automated notification address and any unrelated recipient. Support
   can remain a recipient to retain the group conversation; check that no other
   customer's address or internal discussion is included.
4. Keep the case reference and relevant context. Remove internal-only details,
   verification links, passwords and unnecessary personal information from quotes.
5. Send once and record the action in the website as described below. If you cannot
   select the Support From address, ask the group administrator to fix access; do
   not silently use a personal mailbox as the company address.

Access through the group invitation and verify the account shown when signing in.
The invited account needs its own access; sharing another person's password is not
a handover. The existing Support group does not require a new paid mailbox merely
for documenting this process. Any additional licence cost needs user approval.

## Keep mailbox and website history aligned manually

Email replies are not automatically added to the website conversation, and Gmail
does not automatically change case status or assignment.

After an email or phone exchange, add an **Internal staff note** to the matching
open case. Record only what the next handler needs:

> Contact: [email/phone], [date and time, Nigeria time].
> Summary: [customer's request and response given].
> Outcome: [confirmed / awaiting customer / awaiting workshop].
> Next action: [action and responsible person].
> Reference: [case reference; relevant mailbox subject/date if useful].

Do not paste the same response into a customer-visible reply merely to archive it:
that can generate a second customer email. Do not copy full raw headers, private
links or unnecessary attachments. If a customer-visible update is separately
needed, compose and review that update deliberately.

## Example wording

Acknowledgement:

> Hello [name], we have received your enquiry, reference [reference]. Our team is
> checking [issue/availability] and will contact you with the next step.

Requested visit:

> We have received your preferred visit time of [date/time, Nigeria time]. We will
> check availability and confirm the arrangements. This request does not reserve
> an appointment or vehicle, and no payment has been collected through the website.

Only send a confirmed visit time after the responsible team actually agrees it.
Do not add an invented response deadline, refund promise or guarantee.
