## Proposed customer-facing notice

### Who we are and how to contact us

Allied Autotech Limited operates the Allied AutoTech website. We are responsible
for deciding how the personal information collected through our website is used.
Our workshop is at 133 Stadium Road, beside Kilimanjaro, Port Harcourt, Rivers
State, Nigeria.

For privacy questions or requests, email **support@alliedautotech.com** or call
**08136075567**. Our Operations Supervisor, Newworld Orlu, handles these requests.
Customer-care hours are Monday–Saturday, 8am–6pm, Nigeria time. Our first-response
target is 30 minutes during those hours; messages outside those hours are
acknowledged within 30 minutes of the next opening. Acknowledgement does not mean
that an investigation or privacy request will be completed within 30 minutes.

### Information we collect

- **Enquiries and support:** your name, email, optional phone number, subject,
  message, related service/product/vehicle/branch, and our correspondence and
  handling history. Please avoid including passwords, payment-card details or
  unnecessary sensitive information in messages.
- **Accounts:** your name, email, phone number, password hash, verification and
  sign-in records, and profile information you choose to provide. This can
  include your address, profile photo and vehicle details or images saved in
  your garage. Your account also links to your saved items and requests.
- **Reviews:** the rating, review text and optional vehicle information you
  submit. Approved reviews and the associated public display name can appear on
  the website. A review is subject to moderation before publication.
- **Technical and security information:** information needed to deliver and
  protect the service, including network address, browser/device information,
  request identifiers, sign-in/session records and records of relevant account
  or staff actions. Our hosting and email providers also process delivery and
  security information for their services.

You can browse the catalogue without an account. Contact details are needed to
reply to an enquiry; registration requires the fields marked as required on the
form. Optional profile, photo and garage fields can be left blank. Sending an
enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing.

### Why we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide requested account features,
maintain support history, send essential account/service messages, moderate
submitted reviews, and prevent abuse or unauthorised access.

The proposed processing grounds are taking steps requested by you before a
contract or performing a requested service where applicable, and our legitimate
interests in answering general questions, maintaining secure systems and managing
support. Where a particular activity requires consent, we will request it
separately. We may retain or disclose relevant records where a legal obligation
requires it. We do not use enquiry details for marketing in this launch.

This launch supports browsing and enquiries. Online checkout, paid vehicle
reservations and live payment collection are unavailable. If those services are
introduced, we will explain their additional data use and applicable policies.

### Cookies and information stored on your device

We use a first-party session cookie to keep you signed in securely. Customer
sessions currently expire after at most seven days, with an earlier expiry after
one day of inactivity. Staff sessions have shorter limits. Signing out ends the
session. Security tokens help protect account actions from unauthorised requests.

Selected forms store drafts in your browser's session storage so that navigation
or reloading does not immediately erase entered text. Drafts can contain contact
details and message text you have entered. Password, file-upload and recognised
security-token fields are excluded. Drafts are not a substitute for submitting
the form. Successful submission clears the corresponding unchanged draft;
signing out clears account drafts. Browser settings, restoring a browser session
or clearing site data can affect how long local drafts remain. Use care on shared
devices and clear site data when appropriate.

We have not enabled advertising or marketing tracking in this release. The
workshop map is currently a link to Google Maps. WhatsApp, map and social-media
links take you to services governed by their own privacy terms. If we later add
embedded maps or optional tracking, we will update the notice and any required
controls before enabling them.

### Who receives information and overseas processing

Access is limited to authorised staff and providers needed to operate the
website and communicate with you. These include **DigitalOcean** for backend,
database and image storage, **Vercel** for the website, **Google Workspace** for
business email, and **Resend** for transactional email delivery. Provider
subcontractors may support those services under applicable provider arrangements.

Information can be processed outside Nigeria. The backend is hosted in London;
that does not mean that all website, email, support or provider processing stays
in London. We must document the applicable transfer basis and safeguards for
these arrangements. You can contact us for information about the safeguards
applicable to your information. We do not sell enquiry details or use them for
third-party advertising.

### How long information is kept

For an ordinary enquiry that closes without a sale, booking or complaint, our
approved period is **12 months after closure**. This is measured from closure,
not the date you submitted the message. Related sales, bookings, complaints and
records subject to a legal hold require their own rules.

For customer accounts, profiles, profile photos and saved vehicles, we keep the
information necessary while you actively use your account and related services.
After **24 months without customer activity**, we review the account for closure
and send advance notice. You have **30 days after that notice** to sign in or ask
to keep the account open. Customer activity means a sign-in or genuine customer
interaction linked to your account; staff updates or background emails alone do
not restart this period.

If you do not respond, authorised company staff review and initiate account
closure and removal or irreversible anonymisation of information no longer
needed. Retention deadlines do not automatically delete or anonymise your
records, or close your account. This company-controlled process also applies to
ordinary closed enquiries. You may request deletion earlier without waiting for
the inactivity period. Necessary transaction records, unresolved complaints and
records subject to legal requirements or holds follow their separate rules;
these exceptions do not justify keeping your entire profile indefinitely.

For ordinary complaints, we keep necessary contact details, correspondence,
supporting evidence, actions and outcome records for **24 months after final
closure**. Final closure means agreed actions have been completed and the outcome
communicated; offering a solution alone does not start this period. We do not
treat customer silence alone as **acceptance**. Authorised company staff review the
records and any outstanding obligations or holds before initiating deletion or
irreversible anonymisation. There is no automatic deletion.

If the same issue returns or is disputed, we preserve relevant evidence and
reassess closure and retention. Complaints involving accidents, injury, vehicle
damage, safety concerns, refunds, payment disputes or chargebacks follow separate
rules. Records relevant to threatened legal claims, litigation or regulator
investigations are subject to documented holds with an assigned reviewer and
review date. These exceptions do not permit indefinite retention without a
continuing justification.

Our Operations Supervisor coordinates holds, with the business owner acting as
approver and operational backup. Either may place an urgent protective hold; the
owner confirms its continuing need within two working days. Holds are reviewed
every 90 days, and sooner when circumstances change. The owner approves their
continuation, narrowing or release after checking outstanding obligations and
relevant adviser or provider guidance where applicable. Missed review dates
require escalation, not automatic release. Releasing a hold does not delete your
information or automatically restart its retention period; a separate company
review checks the applicable schedule and any remaining holds.

We retain routine login and security event records for **12 months after the
event**, and ordinary staff-action audit records for **24 months after the event**.
These records help us investigate account-access issues and establish who made
business changes and when. Access is restricted to authorised people who need
the records for security, investigation or oversight. We limit recorded details
to the actor, action, time, affected record and necessary context; passwords,
verification codes, access tokens and unnecessary message contents must not be
logged. Keeping an event record does not extend a session or verification link.

Relevant evidence for an incident, complaint, financial dispute or legal claim
follows its applicable case-retention rule and documented hold where necessary.
Authorised company staff review records due for disposal and initiate removal
only after checking obligations and holds. No automatic deletion or selective
clearing of inconvenient staff history is permitted. Disposal leaves a minimal
record of who authorised it, when and what scope it covered.

We retain a minimal record of a privacy request for **24 months after final
closure** to demonstrate what you asked and how we handled it. For completed
requests, this period starts when the agreed action has been completed and the
final response communicated. For refusals or partial fulfilment, we record our
reasons, actions and final response; any outstanding promised work remains open.
Relevant evidence of a disputed outcome is protected under the applicable hold
process where necessary.

The record includes the request reference, type and date, minimum identifying
details, how and when identity was verified, responsible staff member, decision,
actions completed, final response and any justified exceptions or holds. We do
not retain a duplicate of deleted customer data simply to prove its deletion.
Identity-document copies and full data exports do not automatically receive the
same retention period. We avoid unnecessary identity-document collection and
review temporary verification material and working copies for prompt
company-controlled removal when their purpose ends, subject to applicable holds.
After the request-record period, authorised staff check obligations and holds
before initiating removal of unnecessary records. There is no automatic deletion.

Temporary downloaded customer files, spreadsheets and reports are removed as
soon as no longer needed, within **seven calendar days after the task finishes**,
once necessary business records are safely retained. Where an identity-document
copy is necessary, we remove it within **seven calendar days after verification
is completed**, unless a documented dispute or hold requires it. Temporary
customer-data exports for access requests are removed within **seven calendar
days after successful secure delivery is confirmed**. We keep minimal evidence
of verification or delivery under the relevant record schedule, rather than
retaining unnecessary document or export copies.

For unfinished tasks, we review temporary copies at least every **30 calendar
days**, record why any copy remains necessary and set its next review date.
These rules cover company-controlled attachments, synced folders and duplicate
working copies, subject to documented holds. Disposal is initiated by authorised
staff and recorded minimally; it is not automatic.

Manual database dumps and pre-deployment backups are normally retained for
**30 calendar days from creation**. Before company-authorised disposal, we check
that the supported task is complete, no incident, hold or rollback need requires
the copy, and a suitable replacement recovery path has successful restore
evidence. If those checks fail, we preserve the backup and obtain a documented
owner-approved extension with a new review date and continuing justification.

The company-controlled deletion rules above apply to live business records and
copies under our control. There is a limited exception for documented
provider-managed rotation of recovery backups, expiry of temporary email/log
copies and post-deletion recovery periods. This does not permit the website to
automatically delete accounts or case records, or automatically remove live
uploaded files. Necessary business correspondence follows the relevant record
schedule even when a provider's temporary delivery copy expires sooner.

Removing information from an operational system does not mean every recovery
copy disappears immediately. Recovery copies have restricted access and expire
under their applicable provider schedules. We preserve relevant held evidence
separately rather than relying on short-lived provider copies. An application
hold does not automatically create a hold in our email providers. Before restored
data returns to operational use, the company checks it against approved deletion
and hold records so a restore does not silently reverse completed deletions.
We cannot promise to erase copies already delivered to a customer's own inbox.

**Publication hold:** the ordinary enquiry, account, ordinary complaint, routine
security, ordinary staff-audit, minimal privacy-request, temporary-copy and
manual-backup periods, together with company-controlled deletion, hold governance
and the limited provider-expiry
exception, are approved business instructions. Their operational process must
be verified before this wording is published. Verify actual provider schedules
and settings, group/mailbox copies, media protection and restoration controls.
Complete exceptional complaint/dispute rules, case-specific hold-release
conditions and the working-day/holiday
definition for hold confirmation. No blanket purge is authorised.

### Your choices and rights

You can contact us to request access to your information, correction of inaccurate
information, deletion or restriction where applicable, to object to processing,
or to request portability where the law provides it. Where processing depends on
consent, you can withdraw that consent. Some records may need to be retained for
legal obligations, unresolved matters or applicable legal claims; we will explain
an applicable restriction rather than promising that every request results in
immediate erasure.

We may ask for enough information to verify your identity and locate the relevant
record. Please do not send a password or authenticator code. Customers can also
submit an account deletion/anonymisation request through the account privacy
page; that starts a review and does not instantly erase the account.

You may complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** through its
official website, [ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng/). You do not have to obtain
our permission before approaching the Commission.

We do not make solely automated decisions about granting you credit or accepting
a vehicle purchase in this browsing-and-enquiries release. Automated validation,
security checks and rate limits protect the service; contact Support if they
prevent you from submitting a legitimate request.

### Updates

We will show the version and effective date on this notice and update it when our
services or data practices materially change. An update to this notice does not
by itself enrol you in marketing or replace an approval required for a new use.
