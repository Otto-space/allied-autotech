## Publication checklist

The primary legal reference reviewed is the
[Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, official Gazette hosted by NCC](https://ncc.gov.ng/media/1084/view).
Sections 24–27 cover processing principles, lawful grounds and information given
before collection. Section 27 includes purposes, retention, recipients, rights
and overseas transfers. Sections 34–38 address individual rights. Sections 41–43
require an appropriate, recorded transfer basis and protection assessment. These
requirements inform this review; an owner's operational approval or a provider's
foreign-law clauses alone does not establish the assessment's outcome.

The wording above must not be used to claim that deletion is already automated,
that every request results in erasure, or that all records disappear from every
backup on the anniversary of closure.

Before publication:

1. Review and record the applicable lawful grounds for enquiry handling and
   security operations. Do not turn submission into blanket marketing consent.
2. Review the overseas processing route, applicable provider contractual terms,
   subprocessors, locations and transfer safeguards for this Nigerian business.
   A London database is not a promise that all processing stays in London.
3. Complete the rest of the website notice: account/profile/garage data, security
   and access logs, session/CSRF/private-access cookies, browser-stored form
   drafts, complaints and customer rights/request handling. This short enquiry
   text does not cover those workflows by implication.
4. Define the remaining retention periods, exceptions, mailbox/provider copies,
   backups and operational procedure that will honour the approved enquiry rule.
5. Verify the monitored receiving/replying mailbox, choose a publication version
   and effective date, obtain approval of the complete wording, and place a

business decisions:

   by policy schema and placeholder worker. The approved monthly/yearly/day-based

   Current policy settings accept month-based coarse record types; they do not

   owner-approval/reviewer/due-date fields. The user-linked hold model does not
   directly cover guest-only evidence. Verify controls and the two-working-day
   holiday/calendar definition before claiming the approved process operates.
3. **Privacy requests.** Current intake supports deletion/anonymisation and
   review statuses, not completed fulfilment/final closure or full access/
   correction handling. Do not use review time as the retention starting event.
   A documented, tested manual workflow may support requests; do not claim an
   unimplemented dashboard workflow is live.
4. **Logs.** Persistent login/audit events exist in source and logs have redaction
   rules, but coverage/minimisation and disposal controls still need verification.
   DigitalOcean app components have no configured external log destinations.
   Provider runtime logs are distinct from retained application audit records:
   [Vercel Pro documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/logs/runtime) describes one
   day of runtime logs; [DigitalOcean guidance](https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/app-platform/how-to/view-logs/)
   calls for forwarding to retain runtime logs. Do not promise 12/24 months for
   every provider log or purchase log storage without approval.
5. **Account operations.** Actual provider MFA, Newworld/owner notice delivery,
   least-privilege access and backup account access remain unverified. Recorded
   responsibilities are not proof that notices reach those people.
6. **Copies and recovery.** Verify group/mailbox retention, attachments/downloads,
   local dump encryption, separate media recovery and restoration reconciliation.
   Historical private ACL/CORS evidence does not replace a current dashboard check.
7. **Final notice.** Reconcile all verified flows and remaining exceptional-case
   rules/lawful grounds, then approve exact wording and effective date. Only then
   publish the working privacy page/intake links and perform the separate launch
   checks. This audit does not authorise public cutover or live payments.
