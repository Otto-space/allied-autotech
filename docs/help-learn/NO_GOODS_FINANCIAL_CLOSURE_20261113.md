13 November 2026. Chosen under the owner's bounded D17 delegation. This is not

## Journey and safeguards

An assured current administrator reviews the existing original order, independently
settled affected refunds and actual immutable handover history. Every original unit
must be covered exactly by either actual collection or verified refunded unfulfilled
positions. Current unknown, pending, busy or disputed money remains held. A current
physical pickup review must be truthfully reconciled first. Existing independent
request/approval/transfer/check duties and exact original kobo allocations remain.

The administrator enters a private reason, confirms the actual review, then reviews
the proposed action before explicit submission. The server rechecks identity, scope,
version, settlement and quantities under locks. A UUID protects replay, and an
uncertain response recovers the saved same-version closure before another POST.
Restricted notes, operators, sessions, replay hashes and private proof identities
are absent from customer/guest output. Historical closure is visible with its date,
actual collected/refunded quantities and original/refunded/retained amounts.
Retained payment means historical original coverage, not a new balance or credit.

An order with no actual handover becomes cancelled. If goods were actually handed
over before the remaining goods were refunded, the outstanding obligation becomes
completed through a distinct financial closure record. Earlier partial handovers
remain unchanged, including their original incomplete flag; no final/zero-quantity
handover is invented. Existing completed timestamps remain historical. Only actual
ACTIVE inventory holds may be released. CONSUMED rows, stock quantities, original
prices, VAT, receipts and refunds are retained. Generic cancellation of paid supply
cases is refused so it cannot restock affected consumed goods. Unused codes are
revoked. Immutable SQL evidence and a deferred atomic outcome guard enforce this.
