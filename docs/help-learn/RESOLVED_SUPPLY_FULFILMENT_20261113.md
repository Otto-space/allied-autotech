# Remaining goods after a verified supply refund

13 November 2026, D17 source checkpoint. This is bounded owner-delegated
fulfilment handling, not general returns/warranty approval or purchasing activation.

## Customer journey and truth

The original order keeps its saved quantities, prices, discount and tax. A current
independently settled affected refund and canonical debit exclude only its proved
unfulfilled unit positions. Previously collected goods remain actual physical
history. Unknown, busy, disputed, additional or changed money prevents clearance.
Remaining payment coverage means retained original receipts, including goods
already collected; it is not a new payment due or replacement-order credit.

After current compatibility and actual supply checks, staff can progress the goods
still owed through confirmation, processing and ready. An original active stock
hold is released and replaced with an exact remaining hold before actual consumption.
Original reservation rows and journal history remain. No consumed stock is restocked
by a refund. A separate accepted replacement keeps its own unpaid contract; cancelling
that replacement cannot erase historical acceptance or a verified original refund.

Owned private pickup codes and exclusive current staff review cover only remaining
goods. Each actual handover records exact original positions, excluding refunded
gaps, and its current financial proof. Partial collection leaves the other units
available for a new private authority. A last actual handover completes remaining
goods. No zero-quantity handover, automatic stock return, repricing, fee or forced
credit is introduced. Original-order payment remains held to prevent charging its
original gross again. Payment/provider callbacks still retain actual late captures.

## Exact unfinished work and release boundaries

Fully refunded/no-goods financial closure, cancelled/zero-capture cases and legacy
allocation/whole-order technical-reversal reconciliation still need distinct paths.
Generic confirmed cancellation can restock consumed reservations and must not be
reused for these cases. Keep these states in explicit review until implemented.
Broader returns/warranty remains deferred. Actual Tax ID/CAC/accountant applicability,
verified operating accounts/resources and independent financial/provider/real-role
acceptance remain separate inputs. Publication requires the reviewed integrated
master commit, exact CI checks, byte-matching migration package, fresh verified
backup, off-device retention/PITR and separate populated restore rehearsal before
safe migration/backend/worker readiness and alias checks. No production write or
purchasing activation was performed for this checkpoint.
