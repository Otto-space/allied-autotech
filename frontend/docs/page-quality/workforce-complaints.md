# Managed workforce complaints — milestone 108

Status: locally accepted on 20 October 2026. Route `/admin/workforce/complaints`. The full application redesign goal remains active.

## Outcome

A dedicated searchable queue and selected conversation replace repeated reply forms and raw statuses. Search, priority/status filters, queue paging, selection and history paging persist in validated URL state through reload and Back. Named branches, readable priorities/statuses and recorded acknowledgement, escalation and resolution facts give Customer Service staff the context supported by their active branch assignments and COMPLAINTS permission.

Only customer-visible conversation history is returned. Internal messages cannot influence search; private contacts and unrelated transaction identifiers are excluded. Reply review shows the exact message and visibility, checks fresh access and conversation state, and validates the returned actor/message/version receipt. Changed conversations require explicit review of the retained draft. Unknown outcomes, including post-dispatch 408/429 responses, remain locked through reload until current inspection and independent reconciliation. No automatic resend occurs. Closed/resolved complaints retain history without reply controls. Managed acknowledgement, resolution, priority-change and assignment actions are not supported by these contracts.

## Design gate

The first phone concept was rejected for horizontal record facts; the corrected phone reference uses vertical facts. Accepted images, prompts and the [locked specification](workforce-complaints-oct-concepts/spec.md) are retained in the repository.

Desktop uses an inline filter bar, table, and conversation/reply columns. Phone layouts stack filters, labeled record facts, conversation and response tracking. Recorded dates use Lagos time. Actual content and pagination determine height and control availability.

Native comparison corrected excess desktop toolbar space, a duplicate visible queue heading, response-tracking spacing and selected-row treatment. Real logo/navigation/status colors and flat primary buttons replace generated examples, with these differences documented in the specification. Selection moves focus into the conversation. Capture scripts reset scroll before each full-page and native capture to avoid fixed-header artifacts.
