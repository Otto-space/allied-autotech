# Vehicles concept set

Generated before implementation with the built-in ImageGen tool, then copied into this workspace as WebP. These are visual specifications, not inventory or business evidence. Each original is 1536 × 1024 and was inspected at native resolution.

## Generation briefs

1. **01.webp — listing and introduction.** A readable desktop/mobile Allied AutoTech marketplace board: white navigation and breadcrumb; compact split navy showroom introduction; Nippo display and Quicksand UI; red primary actions; visibly bordered 44px search, filters and sort; removable selected chips; honest current-page count; three example vehicle cards with real-data-shaped fields; Previous/Next pagination. Mobile keeps search visible, wraps chips and stacks cards. Flat screenshots, no device frames. The example vehicles are concept data only.
2. **02.webp — filter controls and states.** A focused desktop expanded panel and mobile drawer, using navy text, red actions, cool greys and explicit borders. Optional exact-name make/model inputs, year, NGN minimum/maximum price, body type, transmission, fuel and featured selection. Three desktop columns, one mobile column, scrolling drawer body and persistent Apply/Clear actions. Include connected price-range error, loading skeleton and no-match recovery states. Do not invent make/model datasets or totals.
3. **03.webp — lower-page continuation.** A coordinated desktop/mobile lifestyle editorial section with family, executive and everyday content; clearly distinguish editorial imagery from current inventory. Follow with a compact navy vehicle-enquiry/WhatsApp band and corporate footer. Use approved Kilimanjaro address, phone/email, Nippo/Quicksand and the official palette. No financing/verification claims, newsletter or fake controls.

## Authoritative implementation decisions

The backend contract and user-approved business information override generated text and control inventions. The output unexpectedly introduced unverified financing and inspection claims, favourite controls, foreign-used categories, required-field asterisks and unsupported footer destinations. These were rejected. Every filter is optional. The original real-data projection and public visibility rules remain authoritative.

Use the existing approved wordmark, navigation, footer, showroom/family assets and lifecycle component. Keep the original lifestyle composition with two photographic scenes and text-based everyday guidance; do not manufacture a third inventory image. Desktop filters are an inline expansion, while mobile uses a modal drawer. There is no implied global count or exhaustive make/model picker.

The final comparison and verification record is maintained in [the Vehicles milestone](../vehicles.md).
