## Audit and design

The existing page had authoritative aggregates, an accessible chart and administrator-only operations disclosure. Its date controls were separated across the header and form; mobile booking tables hid detail controls outside the visible columns. Background read failures were not disclosed, and the refresh timer did not account for an unfinished background read. Monitoring omitted existing worker/alert fields and could retain a reconciliation timestamp after a failed manual read.

Two fresh [desktop and mobile/operations/state concepts](work-overview-oct-concepts/README.md) preceded production changes. The implementation uses one ink summary band, a compact date toolbar, neutral booking statuses, paired chart/progress panels, compact record previews and readable mobile booking rows. The real logo, Quicksand type, navigation and session shell remain authoritative. Generated dates, amounts, health claims and substitute navigation are excluded.

## Contracts and behavior

- `/staff/overview` supplies inclusive Lagos date ranges of 1–90 days. STAFF is limited to its assigned active branch; ADMIN and SUPER_ADMIN receive organisation aggregates and separate verified-settlement/refund totals. No branch filter, trend, profit or net-revenue calculation is invented.
- The work view verifies the returned range and scope before showing metrics. Staff cannot receive finance in its accepted projection, and a branch-scoped response needs branch context.
- `/admin/operations/status` is loaded only when an administrator opens monitoring. Date changes do not filter these current counts. Queue counts, exceptions, latest reconciliation, worker heartbeat flags and the oldest 100 unresolved alerts come from the existing authorized endpoint.
- Worker labels are `Recent success`, `Overdue` and `Not reported`. They do not certify a live process or provider delivery. Missing older response fields are explicitly unavailable; they do not become healthy or zero. The parser rejects contradictory heartbeat records, duplicates, invalid dates and negative counts. Only display fields are retained; internal worker error codes and alert keys are excluded.
- Background failures keep previously loaded data with a visible stale notice. Manual read errors hide prior values. Permission-denied reads and session invalidation remove private data. The refresh timer waits for in-flight reads and backs off failures.
- Monitoring remains read-only. Links open existing authorized review screens; no bulk stock expiry, job retry or financial mutation is introduced.
