# Staff support list specification, gate 76

One Overview > Customer care breadcrumb and compact Customer care header with Refresh
support records. Labelled Enquiries/Complaints category buttons preserve category in
URL; changing category clears incompatible filters/cursors. Grouped status, enquiry
type/complaint priority, administrator branch and assigned-staff filters apply
explicitly. Preserve supported category/filter/cursor/trail state through reload and
Back. Bound pages at25; reject duplicate/unknown/invalid fields before collections.
Do not offer free-text search, total pages, queue aggregates or live chat.

Active current profile/role gates reads; ordinary Staff require their current branch.
Strict full records validate kind, branch, filters, consistent branch/assignee IDs,
unique page IDs and dates. Readable named cards retain full subject/reference/version,
status/type/priority, branch/assignment, customer/contact reference, email/phone,
precise creation/update/resolution/closure and recorded complaint acknowledgement/
deadline/escalation facts. Nulls stay Not recorded/Unassigned. Full text lives in the
conversation; previews are labelled. No portrait or made-up agent/live availability.

Picker outages do not make the queue unavailable. Unresolved selected references
remain visible and must not silently clear applied filters. Branch changes explicitly
clear the assignee draft and reset paging. Staff may choose their own assignment
from the existing picker. Foreign Staff branch URL/response fails closed. Temporary
read failures retain labelled facts/read-only paging; denied reads remove contacts.
Initial error, no results and filtered empty stay distinct. No domain mutation.

Reuse Quicksand30/24px titles,16px sections,13px body/labels,>=44px controls, white12px
panels/cool canvas/subtle rules/restrained red. Desktop grouped records reflow into
complete paired labels/values on narrow screens. Actual Allied shell and navigation
remain. Fresh desktop/mobile Image Gen before UI edits; focused helper/browser/API
and connected read-only role/branch/cursor/privacy checks, seven widths/Axe/native
comparison with >=5 copy/fidelity points complete this gate.
