# Help articles — concept references

Generated and inspected before implementation on 7 October 2026 for `/help/[slug]`.

- [Desktop article](01-desktop-article.webp): compact breadcrumb band, main reading column, topic link, answer/action, preparation notes, related-help rail and contact section.
- [Mobile article](02-mobile-article.webp): single-column reading order and related links below the answer and preparation notes.

The payment article illustrates the template; all twelve real FAQs retain their approved answers. The depicted action is the customer state. Anonymous visitors get the sign-in path, work accounts get only applicable work-account actions, and unknown sessions fail closed.

Use actual bundled Nippo/Quicksand, the existing production logo/header/footer and compact 28–36px display scale. The desktop concept's ink breadcrumb band remains consistent on mobile; the mobile generation's pale breadcrumb and invented logo are not adopted. Preparation items are useful details, not new submission requirements. Do not invent authors, publication dates, reading times, helpfulness totals, business policies or payment outcomes.
